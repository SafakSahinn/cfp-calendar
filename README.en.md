# CFP Calendar

[Türkçe](README.md) · **English**

A website that collects **calls for papers for conferences, workshops and journal special issues** in computer engineering and electrical & electronics engineering in one place, **adds the ones you follow to your calendar automatically**, and helps research groups keep track of their submissions together.

### 👉 https://safaksahinn.github.io/cfp-calendar/

The site is for members only. Sign up for free with your Google account or your email address. The interface is available in English and Turkish.

---

## What can you do?

### 🔎 Find calls
- Calls are refreshed every 12 hours from sources such as IEEE, WikiCFP and ccf-deadlines.
- 31 topics across computer and electrical & electronics engineering: AI, quantum computing, security, signal processing, RF/antennas, power systems, semiconductors and more.
- Browse as a **list**, a **monthly calendar** or a **🗺️ map**.
- Filter by field, topic, date, in-person / online, **IEEE only**, **ranked conferences** (CORE, CCF) or **Q1–Q2 journals** (SCImago).
- **✨ For you:** personal suggestions based on your topics, the calls and series you follow and your past submissions — each with the reason it was suggested.
- **💾 Save a search:** save a filter you use often (e.g. "Q1 signal processing journals"), apply it with one click, and optionally get notified when a new call matches.
- Calls that look predatory (indexing promises, commercial "bulk conference" patterns) are flagged with ⚠ and hidden by default.

### ⭐ Follow calls and get them in your calendar
- Mark a call with **Follow**. When submissions open, the **submission opening**, **abstract** and **deadline** dates are added to your calendar.
- If a deadline is extended, your calendar is updated automatically and you get a message.
- Connect your calendar: **Profile → Connect Google Calendar**.
- **Outlook (including school / work accounts) or Apple Calendar:** use the one-click buttons or the step-by-step guide under **Profile → Calendar subscription (ICS)**. The address is personal; if you share it by mistake, use **Renew address** to invalidate the old one.
- Choose to be reminded **14 / 7 / 3 / 1 days** before deadlines under **Profile → Deadline reminders**.
- Everything you follow is in one place on **My calendar**.
- 🔔 **Phone notifications:** Profile → **Enable notifications on this device**. You are notified when submissions open, a deadline changes or is near — at most 2 summary notifications a day. On iPhone, add the site to your home screen first.

### ✉️ Hear about calls in your field
- Pick your topics under **Profile → My topics**. When a new call appears you get an "Are you interested?" message in **Messages**.
- Answer **I’m interested** and the **conference days** are added to your calendar along with the submission dates.

### 🔁 Follow conference series
- On a call's page, see the **deadlines of previous years** of the series — useful for planning next year.
- **Follow the series** (e.g. ICASSP) and next year's call is followed automatically as soon as it is published.

### 🇹🇷 Conferences in Türkiye
- Choose **In Türkiye** in the filters to see conference series held in Türkiye (SIU, UBMK, ASYU, ELECO, TIPTEKNO, ISMSIT, IDAP, ICHORA): last year's dates and the month the new call usually appears. These conferences are mostly missing from international CFP sources.
- Follow a series whose call is not out yet; you are notified when it is published.

### 💰 TÜBİTAK travel grants
- For researchers in Türkiye: each conference shows which application period of the TÜBİTAK **2224-A** (abroad) or **2224-B** (domestic) conference travel grant you need to apply in.

### 👥 Work together as a group
- Create a group for your lab or team under **Groups** and share the invite link.
- Recommend calls to the group and get a **shared group calendar**. **Comment** under recommended calls (visible to group members only).
- **Track your submissions:** Planning → Submitted → Under review → Accepted / Rejected.
- **After acceptance:** enter camera-ready, registration and presentation dates; get reminders and see them on My calendar.
- **Co-authors:** add co-authors with the invite link on the submission card and update status, dates and shared notes together (your private notes stay yours).
- Optionally share your submission status with your **advisor** (the group owner). Private notes are never shared.
- **Export your submissions:** My calendar → My submissions → **Excel (CSV)** or **Print / PDF**, filtered by year — handy for annual reports and CVs.
- If you allow it, your group mates see that you are applying to the same call: *"3 people from your groups are applying."*

### More
- 📷 Upload a profile photo: it is resized before upload and, after admin approval, visible only to your group mates.
- 🌐 English / Türkçe · 🌙 dark / ☀️ light mode
- 📱 Use it like an app with **Add to Home Screen** on your phone.
- 🔗 Share calls with a link and discover similar calls.
- ⚑ Spotted a wrong date? Use **Report** on the call's page.

## Privacy

- Your profile and group content are not public. Your email address is never shown to other members.
- Your data is never sold or used for advertising.
- Delete your account and all your data at any time under **Profile → Delete account**.
- Details: [Privacy Policy](https://safaksahinn.github.io/cfp-calendar/privacy.html) · [Terms of Use](https://safaksahinn.github.io/cfp-calendar/terms.html) (Turkish and English)

> Dates are collected automatically. Always check the official call page before submitting.
